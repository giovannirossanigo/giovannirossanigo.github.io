# Giovanni Rossanigo
I am a PhD student in mathematics at the Universität Regensburg, working under the supervision of [Prof. Dr. Denis-Charles Cisinski](https://cisinski.app.uni-regensburg.de). I am interested in higher category theory and its applications to algebraic geometry.
More precisely, I like to think about geometry over $\mathbb{F}_1$ and its connection with spectral algebraic geometry.

<p style="margin-top: 2em;">
  I am an organizer of <a href="https://wimregensburg.app.uni-regensburg.de/standard_index.html">Women in Mathematics Regensburg</a>, an initiative at the University of Regensburg for creating an inclusive and supportive environment in mathematics.
</p>
